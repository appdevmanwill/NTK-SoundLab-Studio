Record playback with sound

Some pages assemble images, captions and audio into a moving preview. Record the visible playback when there is no complete video file to download.

  1. Pause the source at the beginning. Use its fullscreen player for a clean recording.
  2. Choose Select playing tab. In Chrome, Edge or Opera, select the source under Tab and enable Share tab audio.
  3. Choose Start recording, return to that tab and press Play after the five-second countdown.
  4. Return here and choose Stop & save when playback ends.

Recording uses the visible tab resolution and re-encodes playback. It does not increase the original quality. Only the surface you choose is recorded; audio stays local. Firefox may offer screen or window capture without sound. For sound, use a Chromium browser.
